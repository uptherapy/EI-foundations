The path

An incident doesn't end when the trucks leave.

Most behavioral health support arrives late and leaves early. We're structured around the full arc — from the planning cycle before an activation through the years of recovery after it.

Phase 01 · Blue sky

Readiness

Pre-incident training and planning so your people already know the playbook when the activation comes.

Phase 02 · 0–72 hrs

Deployment

Rapid needs assessment and on-site stabilization at shelters, EOCs, and staging areas.

Phase 03 · Weeks 1–6

Sustainment

Reset support for crews working extended operational periods. Where retention is won or lost.

Phase 04 · Long recovery

Capacity

Building internal strength so the community and its agencies carry themselves forward without us.
